STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN

1. Project Title

Patterns of Polypharmacy and Multimorbidity Among Adults: A Pharmacoepidemiological Analysis Using NHANES Data.

2. Study Design

Cross-sectional observational study using publicly available NHANES data.

3. Study Population

Adults aged 18 years and above with available demographic, medical condition and prescription medication information.

4. Primary Outcomes

1. Prevalence of prescription medication use.
2. Prevalence of polypharmacy.
3. Prevalence of hyperpolypharmacy.
4. Distribution of medication burden.

5. Secondary Outcomes

1. Association between age and medication burden.
2. Association between sex and medication burden.
3. Association between multimorbidity and polypharmacy.
4. Distribution of commonly reported therapeutic medication classes.

6. Data Preparation

- Inspect datasets.
- Check missing values.
- Identify duplicate participant records.
- Merge datasets using SEQN.
- Validate medication counts.
- Define multimorbidity using eligible chronic conditions.

7. Descriptive Statistics

- Frequencies and percentages for categorical variables.
- Mean and standard deviation for normally distributed continuous variables.
- Median and interquartile range for skewed continuous variables.

8. Inferential Statistics

Potential methods include:

- Chi-square test for categorical variables.
- Independent t-test for suitable continuous variables.
- Mann–Whitney U test for non-normally distributed continuous variables.
- Logistic regression for binary outcomes.
- Appropriate regression models for medication counts.

Final tests will depend on the variable distributions and study objectives.

9. Survey Design

NHANES sampling weights, strata and primary sampling units will be considered for nationally representative estimates and statistical inference.

10. Software

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jamovi, where appropriate

11. Reporting

Results will be presented using:

- Tables
- Graphs
- Prevalence estimates
- Confidence intervals
- Statistical test results
- Scientific interpretation

12. Current Status

Statistical analysis plan under development. Final methods will be determined after data validation and review of the NHANES survey design.
